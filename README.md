## Long Term Aim of the Business
Constant Improvement of Technology : Wirend will be focusing on the transfer of power between a receiver and an AC source. The idea is for a phone device to absorb power modulated to best suit its power specifications, While we plan to fall in line with the Qi standard of a Base Power profile of at least 5 Watts, we hope to develop a way to improve the power output of our product to reach a power profile that extends to a higher power output of 15 Watts. To further boost the power performance, this can be improved to match the proprietary power profile comparable to large multinational corporations such as Apple, Belkin and Samsung, as long as this falls within the range specified by the Qi standard.

Wireless Power Transfer Standardisation: The technology used to implement these power standards, they function based on Near Field communication.With power values that vary as a function of distance, it is commonly considered to be a distance of times the wavelength ( or 0.159 x λ) [Referencing will be done later] from the source surface out to λ (one full wavelength).The other alternative is to use Far Field radiation. Although, Far-Field radiative techniques allow for a greater range in wireless power transfer and at better efficiency, near field provides better power transfer at ranges smaller than the wavelength of the EM wave being transmitted, which is Generally 22 meters for RF, (Radio Frequency) waves[Referencing will be done later]. However, complications arise when dealing with near field techniques. Power across the transmitter antenna becomes non- uniform. This disproportionality across the antenna near field interactions give rise to dangerously unpredictable changes in the power output, which must be constantly monitored to prevent damage to the device or cause serious harm to the user. These problems however do not occur with the implementation of the far field techniques. However, as the name might suggest, these techniques emit radiation that can be harmful to others as well as to the environment. As a precaution, we aim to set up the system in such a way that the device will be placed at a specific zone called the “transition zone”[Referencing will be done later] to utilize the efficiency of the Far field method and the Near field, while minimizing the environmental, and safety hazards faced when dealing with each individual method.

Expansion: Our goal is to develop a wireless phone charging system that meets the standard of the Qi specification. In doing so we hope to develop a system that can implement wireless charging across all types of phones, with an improved power efficiency rating beyond the near region to allow for more user accessibility whilst in the process of charging, on Androids to Apple products. Although we are limited to devices with low power demand like phones which require a low power outputs to charge, the possibility of the development of a universal charger across a larger scope of electronic devices with a greater power demand such tablets and laptops, for example, can be explored in order to safely accommodate these higher power levels and allow users to use the same technology to charge their phones and laptops providing the general public with a multi-gadget charging options to further minimize the inconvenience of having multiple wired chargers.

Product Personalisation: Product personalisation is also another area and long term which can be fully implemented into the company website. Personalisation seems to be one area of interest from survey takers, their feedback entails that they would pay a higher price for a personalised wireless charger which can include an engraving, choice of colour and choice of design.This plans out to be a long term aim which can be implemented into multiple channels providing more income and increasing profitability.
